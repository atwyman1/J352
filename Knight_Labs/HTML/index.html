In a League of His Own: The Best of Sidney Crosby's NHL Career

By: Amelia Twyman

Pittsburgh; April 12, 2026

Sidney Crosby, long-time captain of the Pittsburgh Penguins, is commonly referred to as one of the greatest NHL hockey players of all time.

The Canadian native was drafted to the Penguins in 2005 at just 17 years old and has played for the black and yellow team since.

Over the years, Crosby has won three Stanley Cups, two Olympic gold medals and countless MVP and other major awards. Fans and hockey experts compliment his play-making abilities and impressive agility on the ice.

He is currently in his 21st NHL season, still breaking records and making goals.

In honor of the Penguins clinching a spot in the 2026 playoffs after back-to-back years of them missing the mark, Pens Daily went back in time and picked out a few of Crosby’s career milestones to create a timeline that spans all the way from his rookie season to now.
