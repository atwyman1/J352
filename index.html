Jane Shane: Taking the Entertainment Industry by Storm

Welcome to my fan site for Jake Shane!

Jake Shane is a popular comedian, influencer and podcast host. The 26-year-old, who grew up in New York City and who now lives in Los Angeles, initially gained a following in 2023 from the quirky, improvised skits he would post on TikTok. In the videos, he would reenact niche historical events and pop culture moments based on commented requests, such as John Hancock signing his name on the Declaration of Independence and Jesus paying the bill at the last supper.

Since then, Shane’s platform and fanbase have grown significantly. He now has millions of followers on TikTok and hundreds of thousands on Instagram. He also started a comedic podcast in 2024 called Therapuss with Jake Shane, in which he interviews various celebrities in a conversational format. He is now on season 3 of his podcast, which is streaming on Spotify and Netflix, and some of his most famous guests have included Selena Gomez, Katy Perry, and Joe Jonas. Shane has gone on two live tours around the U.S. for his podcast. The shows doubled as stand-up comedy routines.

Shane is beginning to branch out to other creative outlets as well. He played a small role in season 4 of the hit HBO comedy show Hacks (2021) and recently announced a few other acting projects, including starring in his own Hulu comedy show that is based on his own life. Last month he was named the chief creative officer of a German candy company and he is currently making his Broadway debut in All Out: Comedy About Ambition. Shane still regularly posts on TikTok, feeding the fans who have supported him since the beginning, but his future looks vast as he continues to prove his place in almost every sphere of entertainment.
