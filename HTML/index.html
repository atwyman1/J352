Here's a Breakdown of Consumer Complaints Filed Against Student Loan Servicer Navient, from 2020 to Now

By: Amelia Twyman

April 21, 2026

Consumers have filed nearly 9,000 complaints against former federal student loan servicer Navient over the past six years, according to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau’s complaint database.

Although Navient transferred its federal aid services to Aidvantage, a service unit housed under Maximus Education, in 2021, people have not forgotten about the multiple lawsuits filed against the company and its poor reputation as a federal loan handler.

The graphs below offer a closer look at the timeline, type and source of Navient complaints filed by consumers between January 2020 and April 2026. In 2020, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau received 1,459 files complaining about Navient, but only 732 in 2025. However, complaints reached their peak, at least in the last 6 years, in 2024 at 2,696.

The numbers for this year aren’t sufficient to compare to years past just yet, as complaints are still coming in.

More than 70% of complaints filed against Navient in the time period being studied have been associated with student loan grievances, offering insight into why people were so unhappy with the company’s loan services.

Consumers who file complaints against Navient live in states all across the country, but a majority of them come from America’s most populated states, including California, Texas, Florida and New York.

The state with the most consumer complaints filed is California, at 1,000.

When Navient was still managing federal student loans, it tended to rank as one of the worst servicers among consumers, according to reporting done by the Student Loan Planner. In fact, Navient faced multiple lawsuits during its run as a servicer, as people alleged mistreatment and participation in deceptive practices.

The two bar charts below further break down the types of federal student loan complaints filed by consumers against Navient between 2020 and now.

The most common complaint by far has been “dealing with your lender or servicer,” followed by “struggling to repay your loan.” The fact that consumers have complained the most about Navient’s services directly proves that the basis of the lawsuits filed against the company in previous years are accurate.

Finally, consumers tend to complain about Navient’s handling of private student loans about as much as they do federal ones, with private student loans numbering just a bit more.
